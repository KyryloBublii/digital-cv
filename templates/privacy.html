Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Domain: kyrylobublii.com

What data is collected

This site uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to collect anonymised usage data, but only if you have given consent via the cookie banner. If you declined or have not yet responded, no tracking scripts are loaded.

When analytics are active, GA4 may collect:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Referring URL (where you came from)
  • Browser type, operating system, and screen resolution
  • Approximate country/region (derived from IP address; the full IP is not stored by Google)

No names, email addresses, or other personally identifiable information are collected through analytics. The contact form sends your message directly by email and that data is not retained beyond the email inbox.

Why it is collected

Analytics data is used solely to understand how visitors interact with this portfolio — for example, which projects attract the most interest. It is never sold or shared with third parties for advertising.

Cookies set by this site

  • analytics_consent — stores your accept/decline choice. Expires after 365 days. Set by this site directly.
  • _ga, _ga_* — Google Analytics measurement cookies. Only set after you accept. Expire after 2 years. See Google's privacy policy.

How to withdraw consent

You can withdraw your consent at any time in any of these ways:

  • Clear cookies — delete the analytics_consent cookie in your browser's developer tools or settings. The banner will reappear on your next visit.
  • Withdraw directly —
    — takes effect immediately without reloading.
  • Opt out globally — install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Data processor

Analytics data is processed by Google LLC under Google's data processing terms. Data may be transferred to and stored on servers in the United States. Google is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or to request deletion of any data, use the contact form.
